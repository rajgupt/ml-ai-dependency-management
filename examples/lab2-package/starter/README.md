One notebook: `notebooks/churn_messy.ipynb`. It has everything you've seen in a
real repo — `!pip install` in a cell, two `sys.path.append` lines (one of them
pointing at somebody's home directory), execution counts out of order, two
different definitions of the same feature, and a pickled model at the end.

Your job: turn it into an installable package without changing what the model does.
